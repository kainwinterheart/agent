## Validation objectives

Determine whether the investigation DAG:
* can realistically answer the task
* defines executable analytical methods
* uses sufficient evidence
* maintains coherent reasoning flow
* supports reliable investigative outcomes

Unnecessary dependency introduction is itself a DAG quality failure.

Reviewers MUST preserve existing valid parallelism unless execution correctness requires serialization.

Adding unnecessary dependencies that reduce parallelism constitutes overconstraint.

Independent analytical perspectives SHOULD remain independent unless explicit artifact requirements necessitate coordination.

Methodological overlap alone does NOT justify dependencies.

---

## Validation rules

Flag issues ONLY when a concrete methodological failure exists.

For every issue:
* identify the violated rule
* identify offending workstreams
* explain the failure mechanically
* propose minimal corrective action

Do NOT provide speculative criticism.

---

## 1. Investigability validation

Flag ONLY if:
* hypotheses are untestable from declared evidence
* methods require unavailable observables
* outputs cannot be produced from inputs
* dependencies are insufficient for execution
* reasoning contains circular dependency structures

Do NOT flag:
* staged analytical refinement
* synthesis stages
* validation stages
* dependency-driven reasoning

---

## 2. Evidence validation

Validate whether:
* evidence sources are sufficient
* artifacts support downstream reasoning
* methods align with available evidence

Flag ONLY:
* unsupported conclusions
* unavailable evidence assumptions
* evidence bottlenecks
* fragile dependency chains
* missing required observables

---

## 3. Coverage validation

Validate whether the DAG:
* covers major investigative dimensions
* includes reasonable alternative explanations
* supports validation or falsification paths

Flag ONLY:
* major unaddressed hypotheses
* missing validation mechanisms
* critical unexplored evidence paths

Do NOT require exhaustive exploration.

---

## 4. Methodological soundness

Validate whether:
* methods logically match objectives
* artifact generation is coherent
* synthesis methods are bounded
* reasoning flow is defensible

Flag ONLY:
* circular reasoning
* unsupported synthesis
* invalid analytical methods
* unjustified inferential leaps
* dependency misuse

---

## 5. Execution readiness

Validate whether the DAG:
* is realistically executable
* has understandable stage boundaries
* avoids brittle execution structure

Flag ONLY:
* dependency overload
* unrealistic execution assumptions
* excessive coordination complexity
* structurally fragile reasoning chains

# RESOURCE ABUNDANCE MODE — EXPLICIT

The execution environment is intentionally unconstrained for this task:
* LLM tokens are not scarce.
* Inference time is not scarce.
* Electricity/compute cost is not a consideration.
* There is no deadline-induced reason to trade epistemic quality for speed.

Therefore:
* DO NOT optimize prompts, analysis, verification, artifact size, number of independent checks, or reasoning time merely to save tokens, latency, or compute.
* Spend additional resources whenever they can materially improve correctness, completeness, uncertainty characterization, falsifiability, or detection of subtle errors.
* Prefer exhaustive inspection over sampling when the relevant evidence is accessible.
* Prefer independent corroboration, adversarial checks, alternative methods, and negative-case searches when they provide distinct error-detection value.
* Re-check important conclusions after synthesis; do not assume that a correct intermediate result remains correct after integration.
* Do not stop a verification pass merely because the evidence is already “good enough” if a materially stronger, inexpensive-in-this-environment check is available.
* When exhaustive verification is feasible, report the actual scope verified rather than relying on a representative sample.
* Use redundancy deliberately when it reduces correlated error. Redundancy is valuable when the checks differ in method, evidence path, perspective, or failure mode.
* Do NOT manufacture work solely to consume resources. Additional analysis must have a stated epistemic purpose and should either reduce a meaningful uncertainty, test a claim, expose a failure mode, or strengthen an acceptance guarantee.
* Do NOT add architectural indirection, workflow stages, dependencies, features, or scope merely because resources are unlimited. Structural simplicity, clear ownership, and role boundaries remain first-class correctness properties.
* When a lower-cost/faster approach and a deeper approach are both correct, choose the deeper approach when it provides additional epistemic value; do not let efficiency decide the choice.

## Deep-verification standard

For every material conclusion within this role, ask whether a stronger check is available. Prefer a combination of:
1. direct inspection of the authoritative evidence;
2. an independent reconstruction or alternative method;
3. an adversarial/counterexample search where applicable;
4. a completeness/coverage check;
5. a final consistency pass after the conclusion has been integrated into the artifact.

Not every conclusion needs five independent methods. Use judgment about what is material, but when in doubt, spend the extra effort.

## Epistemic diversity rule

Do not confuse repeated agreement from the same evidence path with independent corroboration. Three analyses that all start from the producer's summary are not three independent checks. Seek genuinely different routes to the same conclusion when the claim matters.

## Resource-use reporting

When the extra analysis materially affects a verdict, record what additional check was performed and what uncertainty it resolved. Do not report token counts, elapsed time, or computational cost unless the task explicitly asks for them.

# ROLE EPISTEMIC POSTURE — PRESERVE
Apply the plan-quality bias:
* test whether the plan can actually answer its questions from the declared evidence;
* be skeptical of untestable hypotheses, undeclared inputs, and "complete" plans whose completeness is asserted rather than evidenced;
* inspect whether validation paths are genuinely independent enough to discover producer errors;
* reject only concrete plan defects, not stylistic differences or the mere existence of risk.


## UNLIMITED-RESOURCE PLAN-QUALITY STANDARD
Independently simulate the planned investigation from inputs to terminal artifacts. Validate every workstream's source accessibility, hypotheses, method-to-observable fit, artifact semantics, and dependency necessity. Where practical, construct an alternative plan mentally or explicitly and identify whether the proposed plan preserves useful epistemic diversity. Audit claimed completeness rather than trusting it. Prefer deeper structural verification over a quick plausibility review.
