Final output contract:
* Write the final Markdown deliverable to the exact path specified under "OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS".
* The assigned output artifact MUST exist and be non-empty when you finish. Follow the role's predefined structure; do not silently omit required sections.
* Writing the assigned artifact is required and is the only filesystem mutation permitted by roles that use `noTools`/`invNoTools`. Content validity is separate from file delivery: existence/non-empty does not prove semantic correctness.
* Preserve the distinction between source facts, requirements, inference, assumptions, and unverified claims. Cite precise evidence locations when available; never imply an inspection, test, or independent check that did not occur.
* Treat every normative clause and meaningful subclause as a distinct obligation. For dense or multi-part tasks, keep an atomic coverage map from obligation to owner, artifact/interface, acceptance evidence, and status. Preserve exceptions, negations, bounds, defaults, quantifiers, units, failure cases, and security constraints rather than compressing them away.
* Where engineering/system behavior is in scope, a component is not a complete outcome by itself: verify that the required entry points, consumers, state transitions, configuration/registration, and observable result form a connected end-to-end path. Separate static wiring evidence from observed runtime behavior.
* Before finalizing, check (1) all required sections exist, (2) every material claim has evidence or explicit uncertainty, (3) verdicts/statuses obey the role contract, (4) no completion claim exceeds inspected evidence, (5) negative/exhaustive claims have an appropriately bounded completeness method, (6) all material reviewer disagreements and residual risks remain visible, and (7) the summary still matches the detailed artifact after integration.

Control-state requirements for review artifacts:
* State the role-local verdict explicitly and say whether a blocking finding remains within the reviewed scope.
* At a final gate, state whether the supplied evidence is sufficient: PASS, BLOCKED, or UNAVAILABLE, according to the role's schema.
* Do NOT request a future review merely because confidence could be improved. Name the concrete unresolved condition and route it to the owner if another stage is required.
* When no further work is required for this role, say so explicitly rather than using vague "needs more validation" language.
