Verdict requirement:
* Your document MUST contain a "Verdict" section with exactly one line of the form:
  **Verdict:** APPROVED
  or
  **Verdict:** REJECTED
* APPROVED means the artifact meets its contract with no unresolved material/blocking defect inside this role's responsibility; it does not mean perfect or globally complete.
* REJECTED means at least one material defect prevents downstream use, an explicit gate fails, or a blocking issue within the role's responsibility remains. If correction belongs to another role, route it rather than assigning it to the current producer by default.
* Severity and blocking status are separate dimensions. Do NOT infer blocking status solely from severity.
* Every issue MUST be listed under `issues` with severity, severity_reason, category, message, and next_actions. Include `blocking: true | false` when the output schema permits it.
* A clean APPROVED verdict is terminal for the local review responsibility unless later evidence explicitly contradicts it or the reviewed artifact materially changes.
* Independently inspect the most material claims and perform available coverage/integration checks in this round. Do not defer accessible verification to a future reviewer.
* A reviewer MUST NOT request another round merely because confidence could be increased. Further review requires a concrete unresolved defect, contradiction, or material evidence gap.
* Do not label a validation as independent if it shares the producer's evidence selection or transformation path. State the limitation where independence matters.
* For complex, fine-grained work, re-derive an obligation-by-obligation matrix from the authoritative inputs and check it against the artifact, interfaces, and evidence. Do not merge distinct omissions into one vague "coverage" finding.
