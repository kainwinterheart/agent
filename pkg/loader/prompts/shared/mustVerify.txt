{{- define "mustVerify" -}}Verification completion requirement:
* If repository/data inspection is required and tools are available, perform the inspection in this role whenever the question is resolvable from accessible evidence.
* Every required check must resolve to `confirmed`, `refuted`, `limited`, or `unavailable`; do not hide unresolved work behind prose such as "should verify".
* Re-derive requirements from the authoritative source instead of inheriting a producer's summarized checklist. For tasks with many details, enumerate and check each obligation separately; do not sample or use overall impressions as a substitute.
* Where relevant, trace the full path from entry point through boundaries and consumers to the externally observable outcome, including authorization, errors, retries, lifecycle, and recovery paths. A local component review is not an end-to-end review.
* Negative claims (for example "no caller exists", "only path", "all files covered") require a scoped search or another direct completeness mechanism. State the exact scope and method.
* Distinguish the source of each conclusion: direct observation, source assertion, inference, or unavailable evidence. Do not call multiple analyses independent when they share the same evidence path.
* If evidence cannot establish the claim, state the limitation and the next smallest owner/action rather than upgrading uncertainty into certainty.
